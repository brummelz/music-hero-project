# Child UX review: ages 12-15 (web version)
Reviewed: Horn Hero web build on branch `html-version`, via 33 screenshots (desktop, tablet, phone; steps 1-cover to 11-settings; I viewed the cover, home, silence, flat, holding, point, level, summary, home-progress and settings shots on desktop/phone and the tablet cover and flat shot), plus `web/index.html`, `web/css/app.css`, `web/js/ui/*.js`, `web/js/app.js`, `web/js/core/feedback.js`. Spec read: FR-16, FR-18, FR-21, FR-25, NFR-01, section 6.1. Earlier review compared against: `docs/ux-reviews/2026-10-10-12-15.md`. Date: 2026-10-11.
Evidence limits: static screenshots only, taken with `?sim` (keyboard simulator, +3 / +35 / -35 cents). No real horn or microphone. Sounds (reference tone, success chime) not heard. The browser microphone-permission prompt was not captured, and neither was the "Let Horn Hero hear you" help card; both are judged from code only. Hold-to-open settings was judged from code (`makeHoldButton`, 1000 ms); no touch interaction was seen. Tablet game shots predate the larger-staff fix, so I do not judge the tablet staff size. Phone shots appear to be about 2x pixel density (780 px wide), so phone CSS sizes below are estimates. Latency (NFR-01) and animation smoothness unverified. Tablet shots 2, 3, 5-11 and desktop 3, 5, 7, 8, 10 were not opened individually; I relied on the matching phone/desktop shots and the shared code for those states. The app was not launched.

## Age 12-15
**Verdict:** On a phone or tablet this teen finds a clean, real-looking tuner they can use on the music stand, but they are stopped at the door by a cartoon cover with a small Start button, then by a one-second press-and-hold just to reach the settings for their own instrument and microphone.

**What works**
- Gameplay (desktop 4, 6; phone 4, 6, 7): state is never colour-only. Flat/in-tune/sharp have a bar position, a lit chip with a symbol (flat sign, check, sharp sign), a banner icon and words. Colour-blind safe. The in-tune chip lights only when in tune and the needle sits on the green.
- "Hearing DO / C4" on the banner (flat state) gives a teen real information about what the app hears, and "2.0 / 3.0 s" gives the exact hold time. This respects the player, matches what teens want, and is new since the desktop review.
- Silence (phone 3): pale gauge, dashed needle, dashed ring, "Take a breath and play your Do! / Listening...". Calm, not an error.
- Reset cue is now in the code: `TRY_AGAIN` "So close! Start the hold again." with a restart icon, shown only after at least 1 s of hold (earlier finding 5, fixed in logic; not visible in any screenshot, unverified).
- Level complete (desktop 9, phone 9): "Amazing! All 3 stars (0 restarts)." and, for fewer stars, "You had N restarts. Keep it to X or fewer to win all 3 stars." (`starReason`, threshold read from config). This fixes earlier finding 3.
- Layouts adapt: desktop is a two-card home; phone is one column with big note buttons (about 105 css px wide, 95 tall), a full-width "Start practice", and a pill-shaped level list with big "Play" tap targets. All interactive controls on Home and Game are at least 48 css px (`--touch`), above the common 44 px minimum.
- The hold ring on the staff note, the counter and the green note colour all say "keep going", and the star points row (0 / 10) with dots is clear on desktop.
- Strong focus outline (4 px yellow) on buttons, selects and inputs in the CSS: earlier finding 14 addressed (visible only as the yellow ring on the focused select in settings shots).
- Messages are positive throughout; nothing says wrong, failed or lost (FR-16, FR-18 respected). Reset progress needs a confirmation (FR-25).
- Settings now groups Playing / Microphone / Sound, and the live test and "Hearing" readout (note, Hz, cents) is a real tool for a teen.

**Problems**
1. [Major] Cover on phone (phone 1) - "The Start Game! button is a thin pill in the middle of a picture. I can hardly tap it with my thumb." The cover image is a fixed 1600x1195 landscape illustration; the button is a hotspot at 9.9% of image height, which on a 390 px-wide phone is only about 29 css px tall (estimate), and the picture floats in a huge empty band above and below. Below the 44-48 px target the rest of the app uses, on the one screen that also triggers the microphone permission. Fix: on narrow or portrait screens, render the Start button as a real full-width HTML button (at least `--touch` tall, more is better) below or over the image instead of a percent-positioned hotspot, and let the image fill more of the screen (crop or use a portrait cover).
2. [Major] Cover, all sizes (desktop 1, tablet 1, phone 1) - "A boy, a squirrel, a bunny and a smiling microphone, and 'The Musical Adventure!' I'm not opening this on the bus." Same finding as earlier finding 1; NOT fixed, and it is worse on the web: a teen opens a URL in public, on a phone, with sound possibly on. Fix: show the cover only on the first visit (store a "cover seen" flag in the profile, FR-20) or add a calmer cover variant; returning users go straight to Home. The mic permission would then need a quiet entry point (for example a "Start" on Home or the first time they press Start practice). Spec decision needed (see below).
3. [Major] Cover Start doubles as microphone permission (`web/js/app.js` begin(); phone 1) - "I tapped Start Game and my browser asked to use my microphone. Why does a game need my mic? Was that a trick?" The child gets a system prompt with no warning, no explanation of what it is for, and no preview, and the text for the denied case ("Tap Try again and choose Allow") is only revealed after the fact. A teen who taps "Block" by reflex (or whose school device blocks it) lands on the help card, which is calmer, but the only action is "Try again": no way to see the app without a microphone or continue. (Card never captured; unverified visually.) Fix: put one plain line above/under Start, for example "Horn Hero listens to your horn. Allow the microphone when asked.", with a microphone icon; keep the denied card positive (FR-16), and add a secondary way to look around. Keep the spec's behaviour (the same click starts audio and asks for the microphone) unchanged.
4. [Major] Settings button on touch (all game and home screens; `makeHoldButton`, `HOLD_MS = 1000`) - "I want to change the microphone or the tolerance, and tapping the gear does nothing except flash 'Hold to open settings'." The hold-to-open guard is aimed at small children but it is the only way for a 12-15 year old (who owns the horn and needs the tolerance and microphone controls) to reach settings; the gear's aria-label also reads "Settings (hold)". On a touch screen a held press also fights the browser's own long-press behaviour (context menu is suppressed; text selection and scroll gestures on some phones are not tested, unverified). Hold is also mandatory mid-game, interrupting the game (pauses, then resumes with the hold restarted). Fix: keep it as the default for young ages but make it a setting or age-aware: for 12-15, a single tap (or a short 300-400 ms hold) opens settings. The hold time should be a config value, not hard-coded. Spec note: FR-25 / section 6.1 do not define hold-to-open, so this behaviour needs a spec line either way.
5. [Major] Settings dialog on phone (phone 11) - "The panel is taller than my screen, and I can't see Done or Reset progress." The dialog is capped to `100dvh - 24px` and scrolls, but the screenshot shows the form cut at "Play a long note: the bar should move..." with the Sound section, Reset and Done below the fold, and no visible scroll cue. A teen may think it is stuck. Also the same dialog shows the whole Microphone troubleshooting block (live test, Hz, filter warning) before volume. Fix: sticky "Done" at the bottom of the dialog and an X/close at the top right; consider a collapsible "Microphone test". Scroll behaviour itself is unverified.
6. [Major] Settings copy (phone 11, desktop 11) - "'For grown-ups'? I'm 14 and this is my horn." Unchanged from earlier finding 7 and still patronising for this band; "Normal (±15 cents)" with no explanation of cents also remains. Fix: "Changes are saved straight away." and one line under tolerance: "How close to the note you need to be. Smaller is stricter."; label the pause checkbox plainly ("When the note slips, keep my hold time instead of restarting"). Tolerance presets stay config/settings (FR-21).
7. [Major] Point scored, banner (phone 7) - "It says 'Great job! +1 point!' but the counter underneath reads 0.5 / 3.0 s, and the ring is already filling again. Did it count?" The celebration banner and the next hold's timer show together, so the teen sees two contradictory messages at once (score says 1 / 10 but the counter says 0.5 s). Fix: while the celebration is showing, hide the hold counter (or show "+1" instead), and keep the ring empty until the celebration ends, or make clear that the next hold starts after the chime. Unverified whether the 0.5 s is simulator artefact (key still held), but a real player holding the long note will hit the same overlap.
8. [Minor] Practice and level banner wording, all sizes - "In tune banner says 'Perfect! Keep it steady...' but I'm 15 cents off." Earlier finding 4 half-fixed: the summary now says "steady", but the in-game banner (`MESSAGES.in_tune`) still says "Perfect!" for anything inside the tolerance. Fix: "In tune. Keep it steady..." (data-driven wording per age band; FR-18 defines messages by state only, so a copy change needs a spec note).
9. [Minor] Practice summary (desktop 9 shape, `showPractice`) - "You held 10 steady Do notes. Super playing!" and a white card with just a number row. Earlier finding 4 (no stats, empty card) remains; the web summary is still the same single card, with a huge empty white area on phone (phone 9: card is 1640 px tall for 5 lines). Fix: add one stats line (restarts, closest to centre), and let the card fit its content; "Super playing!" can drop for this band.
10. [Minor] Praise tone (feedback.js; "Super playing!", "Great job! +1 point!", "Amazing!") - unchanged from earlier finding 12; leans primary school. Fix: per-age message sets, for example "Held it. +1." Keep short and positive.
11. [Minor] Game screen on phone (phone 4, 6) - "Half the screen is an empty staff card." The staff card takes about 680 px of 1690 and the note sits in its middle with a lot of white around it, while the gauge and banner text are squeezed; the hold counter ("2.0 / 3.0 s") and "Hearing DO / C4" drop to a small second line. Fix: give the gauge and banner more height than the staff in portrait phone, and put the counter next to the ring. (Tablet portrait: not judged; shots predate the fix.)
12. [Minor] Header on phone (all game shots) - the instrument chip ("Horn in F") is hidden under 600 px, but the staff caption still says "Written pitch · Horn in F", so the information is present in the game; on Home, the teen cannot see which instrument is set without opening settings (and settings needs a hold). Fix: show the instrument in the home header or beneath the title on phones.
13. [Minor] Home on desktop (desktop 2) - a large empty band under the cards, unchanged from earlier finding 10. The "Horn in F" chip still looks like a button (outlined, same style as the gear) but does nothing; earlier finding 9 remains. Fix: draw it as a flat tag, or tap it to open the instrument setting.
14. [Minor] Sound - no mute or volume control on Home or Game; the only volume slider is inside the held settings dialog. Earlier finding 13 remains; on a phone in public the success chime plays on every point. Not heard (unverified). Fix: a mute toggle in the game header (stored as a setting).
15. [Minor] Simulation pad arrows - not seen by real players (only with `?sim`), but its "↓ Flat / ↑ Sharp" is the opposite of the banner arrow (up = play higher). Earlier finding 6 is otherwise fixed: the gauge now uses flat/sharp signs instead of arrows. Fix: only if the pad is ever shown to testers.
16. [Minor] Back button (game screens) - one tap leaves the level and nothing in the screenshots shows a confirmation; a thumb on a phone that is also holding a horn can hit it by accident. Whether progress is lost is unverified. Fix: confirm only when a level is partly done, or return to the same state on re-entry.
17. [Minor] Orientation - phone landscape CSS exists (trimmed chrome) but no screenshot of it, unverified. A horn player on a stand will often use landscape.

**Would this child come back tomorrow?** Maybe. The in-game tuner is clear and honest and now tells them what the app hears, and the stars say why. But the cover, the one-second press just to reach settings, and a hard-to-tap Start on a phone make it feel like a kids' app that fights them; a teen with a plain tuner app one tab away will switch.

## Cross-age summary
Only 12-15 was reviewed in this pass, so cross-age statements are predictions.
- Issues ranked for this band: (1) the cover (look, forced every visit, thin Start button on phone, mic prompt without explanation); (2) settings reachability (1 s hold, tall dialog on phone, "For grown-ups"); (3) the point-scored banner showing a counter again; (4) "Perfect!" wording and bare summary; (5) no mute.
- Conflict: hold-to-open settings protects 3-8 year olds from changing tolerance or microphone but blocks 12-15. Recommended resolution: keep the hold as default, and let a grown-up or teen switch to a normal tap in settings (stored as a setting, hold time in config), or default by age.
- Conflict: cartoon cover (right for 3-8) vs babyish for 12-15. Resolution: first-visit only, or a calmer variant; do not remove for the young bands.
- Conflict: large touch targets help every age, so raise the cover Start and the settings gear to at least the 48 px baseline everywhere; no conflict, no downside for teens.
- Spec flags (do not change silently): section 6.1 says the cover's Start Game also asks for the microphone, so the cover cannot simply be skipped without defining where permission is asked (findings 2, 3). Hold-to-open settings is not in FR-25 or section 6.1 (finding 4). FR-18 defines messages by state only, no age variants (findings 8, 10). "Perfect!" in the in-tune banner is inconsistent with an in-tolerance, not exact, test (FR-21). FR-16 is respected in all screenshots reviewed. The mockups in `docs/design/*.dc.html` were not compared in this pass.

# Child UX review: ages 12-15
Reviewed: Horn Hero v0.1 app build, via 12 screenshots (cover loading, cover start, home, practice silence/flat/sharp/in-tune holding/point scored, level 2 holding, level complete, practice complete, settings), plus ui/theme.py, ui/cover_screen.py, ui/game_screen.py, ui/home_screen.py, ui/settings_dialog.py, audio/sfx.py and core/feedback.py. Spec read: FR-06, FR-09, FR-14, FR-16, FR-18, FR-22, FR-23, NFR-01. Date: 2026-10-10.
Evidence limits: static screenshots and code only, no live interaction. No real horn or microphone: the screenshots use the keyboard simulator (offsets of +3 / +35 / -35 cents). The success chime was not heard (code shows a 4-note arpeggio at 0.6 x volume). Responsiveness and animation are inferred from code only (gauge about 15 ms refresh with smoothing, hold ring over 3 s, 150 ms grace); NFR-01 latency is unverified. The visual behaviour after the hold timer resets (leaving the green zone mid-hold) was not captured in any screenshot (unverified). The app was not launched by the reviewer.

## Age 12-15
**Verdict:** A teen would find the tuner and practice screens clear and respectful ("this is a real tuner"), but the cover screen and the results screens make the app feel like a kids' game, and the stars give no information to work with.

**What works**
- Practice screen (05-08): the gauge is not colour-only. Each state has a position on the bar, a labelled chip (Flat / In tune / Sharp) with an arrow or check, and a banner with icon plus text. A colour-blind teen can still read it.
- "Written pitch · Horn in F" and "Hold for 3 seconds" are stated plainly on the staff card. Real notation and a real tuning gauge treat the player as a musician.
- Silence (04) is a calm pale gauge, a dashed ring and "Take a breath and play your Do! / Listening...". It does not look like an error.
- Hold feedback (07, 09) is a live "1.9 / 3.0 s" counter plus a filling ring, so the teen can see exactly what is being measured. Point scored (08) is short: ring closes, star icon, "+1 point!", progress pill moves to 1/10.
- Messages are short and positive (FR-18). Nothing says failed or wrong (FR-16). Flat and sharp banners are soft pink and yellow, not alarming.
- Home (03): one obvious path per mode, the next level highlighted with a Play button, locked levels clearly dashed, big text, good contrast. Back and Settings are always on the game screen, so the teen keeps control.
- Settings (12) is reachable from the game via the gear and saves instantly, with a confirmation on Reset progress. Tolerance, sensitivity, an input level meter and a pause-vs-restart option are all there.

**Problems**
1. [Major] Cover (01, 02) - "A cartoon boy, a squirrel, a bunny, a smiling microphone and 'The Musical Adventure!' - this is for little kids, and I won't open it in front of friends." The cover is the first impression and the most childlike thing in the app, far more so than the clean Home and Practice screens. Fix: keep the cover art for the young bands, but show it only on first launch (then go straight to Home), and/or offer a calmer variant for this band (logo plus horn illustration, no mascots, no "Musical Adventure!" subtitle). Needs a spec decision (see below).
2. [Major] Cover loading (01) - "I have to wait for a fake 'Warming up the horn...' bar every time, then press Start Game!" The loading bar is a fixed 1.6 s timer (`_LOADING_MS` in ui/cover_screen.py), not real work, followed by a second click. For a returning teen these are two pointless steps before playing. Fix: tie the bar to real start-up work (audio and mic ready) and skip it when already done; let any key or click on the cover go straight to Home; remember "cover seen" in the profile.
3. [Major] Level complete (10) - "I got 2 stars. Why? What do I have to do to get 3?" The screen says "Try again to collect all 3 stars" but shows no miss count and no rule (0-2 misses = 3 stars, FR-23). Teens want to master things and will otherwise think the rating is arbitrary. Fix: add one plain line under the stars, for example "4 restarts this level. 2 or fewer for 3 stars.", using the miss count (FR-22) and thresholds read from config, not hard-coded.
4. [Major] Practice complete (11) - "'You played 10 perfect Do notes. Super playing!' That's it? And a huge empty white card." There are no stats (best hold, restarts, time) and nothing to beat next time. "Perfect" is also inaccurate: tolerance is +/-15 cents and the teen saw the needle was not at the centre mark; teens notice when praise does not match what they did. Fix: replace "perfect" with "steady" or "in tune"; add a small stats row (restarts, closest to centre, time); resize the card so content is not floating in empty space. Keep it positive (FR-18) and free of failure wording (FR-16).
5. [Major] Practice and level screens (05-07), hold reset - "I was at 1.9 seconds, wobbled out, and the counter dropped to 0.0 with no explanation." This is the "note held but timer reset" case that older children call unfair. Code has a 150 ms grace period and a Pause option hidden under "Advanced" in Settings, but the main screen gives no cue (unverified visually). Fix: when the timer restarts, show a brief non-judgemental cue (ring drains back to empty and the banner says "Back to the start, you've got this" for about a second) so the teen sees why. In Settings, move the Pause-vs-Restart choice out of "Advanced" and explain it in plain words. Hold constants stay in config/settings.
6. [Minor] Game screen (05, 06) - arrow meanings conflict. The gauge label says "↓ Flat" (describes the pitch) but the banner icon for flat is "↑" (what to do). A fast-reading teen sees opposite arrows for the same state. Fix: use one meaning for arrows, or add a word ("Higher" / "Lower") next to the banner arrow. Keep icon and text, never colour alone.
7. [Minor] Settings (12) - "For grown-ups." tells a 14-year-old who owns the horn that this is not for them, which reads as patronising. "Cents" is unexplained in "Normal (±15 cents)". Fix: reword to "Changes are saved straight away." and add a line under tolerance: "How close to the note you need to be. Smaller is stricter."
8. [Minor] Settings (12) - native Windows spin box, drop-downs and checkbox clash with the rounded look of the other screens, and the preset and custom tolerance controls sit side by side unexplained. Fix: restyle to the app theme and group into Tuning / Sound / Microphone.
9. [Minor] Home (03) - the "Horn in F" chip looks like a button but is a label (ui/home_screen.py), so a teen who clicks it gets nothing. Fix: make it open the instrument setting, or draw it as a flat tag.
10. [Minor] Home (03) - large empty areas under the Practice hint and under "Leaps" look unfinished. Fix: tighten the layout or use the space for a recent-progress line.
11. [Minor] Game screen (04-09) - the target note label ("DO / C") sits left of centre while the staff and gauge are centred, so the top bar looks slightly off. Fix: centre it.
12. [Minor] Praise tone - "Super playing!", "Great job! +1 point!", "You did it!" are fine alone but together lean primary-school. Fix: make message sets data-driven per age band, with neutral phrasing for this band ("Held it. +1.", "Nice and steady."). Spec question (FR-18 has no age variants).
13. [Minor] Sound - the chime plays on every point (10 times per practice session) at 0.6 x volume, and the volume slider (default near 80% in 12) is only in Settings. In a shared room or at school this could embarrass a teen. Not heard (unverified). Fix: a mute toggle on the game header, or a softer chime option, stored as a setting.
14. [Minor] Keyboard and focus - no visible focus ring on Back or Settings in the gameplay screenshots (unverified in the live app). Fix: confirm focus outlines on all buttons for keyboard users. The "Simulation: hold Space..." hint appears only for the simulated source per code, so it is not a real-horn issue.

**Would this child come back tomorrow?** Maybe. If they get past the cover, the tuner is clear and fair-feeling and the levels give something to improve. With a cover that looks like a game for 6-year-olds and stars they cannot interpret, they may reach for a plain tuner app instead.

## Cross-age summary
Only the 12-15 band was reviewed in this pass, so cross-age ranking is limited.
- Ranked issues for this band: (1) cover look and forced fake loading, (2) opaque star rating, (3) bare and slightly inaccurate practice-complete screen, (4) hold reset gives no cue, (5) mixed arrow meaning.
- Likely conflict: the cover mascots and high-praise copy suit ages 3-8 but are babyish at 12-15. Recommended resolution: keep the playful default, make the cover first-launch-only or skippable, and add age-appropriate message sets, rather than redesigning the app.
- Likely conflict: big simple messages (3-5, 6-8) versus detailed stats (12-15). Resolution: one-line stats in smaller text beneath the main reward on results screens.
- Spec flags (do not change silently): FR-18 defines messages by state only, with no age variants. No requirement covers skipping or varying the cover. FR-23 star thresholds are not shown to the player; displaying them needs a spec decision. FR-16 (no failure state) is respected in all screenshots reviewed. No design/spec disagreement was checked against docs/design mockups in this pass (not read).

# Child UX review: ages 6-8
Reviewed: Horn Hero v0.1 running build (12 screenshots driven by the keyboard simulator), plus ui/*.py and core/feedback.py, 2026-10-10
Evidence limits: static screenshots and code only, no live interaction. No real horn or microphone input (a real horn wobbles in pitch; the simulator does not). The success chime cannot be heard. Animation and timing are inferred from code only (gauge ~15 ms tick with smoothing, hold ring fills over 3 s, 150 ms grace). The behavior after the pitch leaves the green zone mid-hold (ring reset, grace) was not captured: unverified. Hover, press and focus states were not captured: unverified.

## Age 6-8
**Verdict:** The child feels welcomed by the cover and knows what to play, and the in-tune moment is clear and happy. The reward moments (point scored, level and practice complete) are much smaller and plainer than the cover promises, and one tap on the gear can reach Settings and "Reset progress".

**What works**
- Cover (01, 02): friendly character, mascots, one big "Start Game!" button, "Warming up the horn..." explains the wait. Clear first action.
- Home (03): one question ("What do you want to play today?"), two large cards, large Do / Ré / Mi tiles with letters as secondary text, big "Start practice" button, only the current level shows "Play", locked levels are dashed and labelled "Locked" with a lock icon (not color alone). Total stars chip is easy to read.
- Practice (04-08): the target is huge ("DO / C") and uses solfege first, which fits this age. The silence state (04) is calm: "Take a breath and play your Do!" with a music icon and "Listening...", no error look.
- Flat / sharp / in tune are not color alone: marker position, arrow or check icon, the highlighted label pill (Flat / In tune / Sharp) and the message all agree (05, 06, 07).
- Hold progress is a ring around the note that fills green, with a "1.9 / 3.0 s" read-out. The ring is a good, literal "it is filling up" cue (07).
- Messages are short, positive and name a direction (FR-18). No failure wording in any captured screen (FR-16).
- Level complete (10): large stars, the empty star is dashed rather than crossed, "Level 3 unlocked" is an extra win, "Next level" is the obvious button.

**Problems**
1. [Major] Practice screen, score pill (top right, 08) - "Something happened... was that it?" The only change for a scored point is one tiny dot filling in the pill ("1 / 10"), the text changing to "Great job! +1 point!" and a small star in a white circle. A 6-8 child works hard for 3 seconds and gets a very small reward, after the cover set up big expectations. The chime is unverified. -> Fix: on a point, pop a large star (or the mascot) over the staff card for about 1 s and fly it into the score pill; make the score pill dots about 2x larger or use star icons, with a bounce on the newly filled one. Keep the existing 1.2 s celebrate timing and the volume setting.
2. [Major] Practice complete screen (11) - "That's it? Where are my stars?" White empty card, plain text "You did it!", two buttons. No stars, no character, no visual link to the 10 points earned. It is the "I did it!" moment for this age and the flattest screen in the app, unlike the cover. Level complete (10) is better but also has no character or motion. -> Fix: add the boy-with-horn / mascot art from the cover and a row of 10 filled stars (or one big star) to screen 11; add a small star burst to screen 10. Keep the text short.
3. [Major] Settings gear (practice top bar 04-08, home 03) - A curious child taps the round gear next to the score pill (about 90 px, same weight as the back arrow) and reaches Settings and "Reset progress...". "For grown-ups" is small text a 6-8 reader may skim. Reset does have a Cancel-default confirmation (settings_dialog.py), which helps, but tolerance, instrument and the pause-timer option can still be changed by accident mid-practice. -> Fix: make the gear smaller and lower contrast than the back arrow, and open Settings only on press-and-hold (about 1.5 s) or a simple grown-up gate. Consider removing the gear from the practice top bar (the spec text I searched does not require it there; confirm before removing).
4. [Major] Practice screen, feedback bar (05, 06) - "Push higher? Come down? Do I move my horn?" 6-8 children are literal. "Push a little higher!", "Relax and come down." and "Lift the note up." can be read as moving the horn or arm, not changing the sound. The arrow in the white circle helps, but it is not visibly linked to the gauge arrows. The "Push a little higher" wording is quoted as an example in the spec (SPECIFICATIONS.md line 86, FR-18 at line 87), so this is a wording decision to confirm with the teacher, not a silent change. -> Fix: keep the arrows and talk about the sound, e.g. "Almost! A little higher sound!" / "A little lower sound, you can do it!"; enlarge the arrow icon to about 36 px and match the gauge arrow. Confirm wording with the music teacher.
5. [Major, inferred from code, unverified live] Practice screen, feedback bar and gauge - A beginner horn wobbles. Each 15 ms tick re-evaluates the zone (game_screen.py _tick -> _show_feedback) and the bar color and sentence swap immediately. A child near the edge of green may see the bar flip between green, yellow and pink several times a second, which reads as flicker and as being told off. The 150 ms grace period protects the timer, not the message. -> Fix: add a minimum display time (about 400 ms) before switching away from a message, as a config constant, and show the in-tune state as soon as it is entered.
6. [Minor] Practice screen, gauge zones (05, 06) - The far-flat and far-sharp ends are saturated red. Red means "wrong" to a young child even though the text is kind. -> Fix: soften the outer zones to coral or purple; keep position, arrow and label as the primary cue.
7. [Minor] Practice screen, staff card captions (04) - "Written pitch · Horn in F", "TARGET NOTE" and "Hold for 3 seconds" are small and use words a 6-8 child does not know. -> Fix: move "Written pitch · Horn in F" to grown-up info, change "TARGET NOTE" to "PLAY THIS NOTE", enlarge "Hold for 3 seconds" and pair it with a timer icon.
8. [Minor] Practice screen, hold read-out (07) - "1.9 / 3.0 s" needs decimal reading, and the ring already shows progress. -> Fix: show whole seconds (1, 2, 3) or remove it for this age.
9. [Minor] Level complete (10) - "Try again to collect all 3 stars." beside a dashed empty star leans on what is missing. FR-16 is respected but the tone is weaker. -> Fix: "Great playing! Can you get all 3 stars next time?" and keep Next level as the primary button.
10. [Minor] Home (03) - After the playful cover, home has no character and looks like a settings page. A first-time child does not know whether to pick Practice or Adventure. The "Horn in F" chip looks like a button but is static. -> Fix: add the mascot to the header, mark Adventure as the suggested first choice for a new profile, give the instrument chip a flat label style.
11. [Minor] Practice screen, silence state (04) - The staff ring is a pale dashed line and the gauge is washed out. Calm is right, but the child may not realize the app is waiting for them. -> Fix: add a gentle pulse on the dashed ring while listening (respect reduced motion).
12. [Minor] Settings (12), for adults - Two controls for the same tolerance (preset dropdown and +/- cents box), "cents" jargon, an empty "Input level" bar labelled "Quiet". Fine for grown-ups. -> Fix: add one plain-language line under "How exact the note must be".

**Would this child come back tomorrow?** Maybe. The cover and the clear Do/Ré/Mi targets win them over and the in-tune feedback is easy to read. Without a bigger reward when a point is scored and a real celebration at the end, the loop feels like a task. Fixing items 1-2 would move this to yes.

## Cross-age summary
Only age 6-8 was reviewed.
- Items 1, 2 and 5 (reward size, flat end screen, message flicker) will likely affect 9-12 too; 3-5 and 12-15 not assessed.
- Probable later conflict: large mascot art and star bursts suit 6-8 but may feel babyish for 12-15; resolve with a calmer celebration option rather than removing the reward.
- Spec flags: feedback wording is quoted in FR-18 / Screen 2 ("Almost there! Push a little higher!"), so Major item 4 needs the spec owner's agreement. FR-16 (no failure state) holds in all captured screens. Item 3 (gear on the game screen) is not mandated by the spec text searched; confirm before removing. The 3 s hold, 150 ms grace and tolerance values must stay settings/config constants.

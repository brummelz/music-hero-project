# Child UX review: ages 3-5
Reviewed: Home screen Practice card, 7 note buttons in a 4+3 grid. Files: `ui/home_screen.py` (`_build_practice_card`), `ui/theme.py` (`QPushButton#note`), `web/css/app.css` (`.note-choices`, `.note-choice`, phone block near line 404), `web/js/ui/screens.js`, `web/index.html`. 2026-10-23.
Evidence limits: The app could not be run and no screenshots exist. Everything below comes from code and CSS. Rendered sizes, the empty 8th cell, the web phone layout and the Qt button layout are unverified. I did not re-read the mockups in `docs/design/` for this change, and I did not check that they show 7 notes.

## Age 3-5
**Verdict:** A grown-up will have to pick the note. The buttons are big enough to hit, but a pre-reader sees seven near-identical white boxes with words on them and has no cue for which one to press or why.

**What works**
- The buttons are large (Qt min-height 100px, web 84-120px) with thick 4px borders and 24px rounded corners. That is a generous target for poor motor control.
- Gaps are 12px, which helps against mashing neighbours.
- The selected state changes both fill and border. On the web it also adds a check mark (`::after`), so selection is not color alone.
- The first note (Do) is pre-selected, so "Start practice" always works. There is no way to get stuck with nothing chosen.
- Nothing on this card sounds like failure or loss (FR-16).

**Problems**
1. [Major] Home, Practice card - "I can't read these. Which one do I press? They all look the same." Seven buttons with only solfège words and letters give a pre-reader no distinguishing picture. Going from 3 to 7 choices makes this worse: the child can no longer tell notes apart by position, and the order (Do to Si) is meaningless to them. Likely behavior: tap the first or biggest-looking button, or ask a grown-up. -> Fix: give each note a distinct non-text cue next to the name, such as a color plus a shape or a picture, or show a small staff dot at its height. Do not use color alone. Keep the text for adults and older bands. Alternatively, preselect Do and make "Start practice" the one obvious action, with the other notes as secondary.
2. [Major] Home, Practice card - "What do I do first?" Choosing 1 of 7 notes before pressing start is a decision a 3-5 child cannot make. The card text ("Pick one note and play it 10 times", "Hold the note steady for 3 seconds to score a point") is unreadable to them. 10 repeats at 3 seconds each is also far beyond a 3-5 attention span. -> Fix: do not add any child-facing flow that depends on reading here. Keep the default note preselected, and make sure the large Start button is visually dominant. Consider a spoken or animated cue, but that is a new feature, so flag it to the spec owner. For 3-5, Adventure level 1 (3 notes) is the better entry. Check that Adventure is the visually primary choice.
3. [Major] Home, Practice card (web phone, `max-width: 600px`) - "The buttons got skinny." With `repeat(4, 1fr)` on a roughly 360px phone, each button is only about 65-70px wide. Height stays at least 84px, so the shape is tall and narrow. A 3-5 hand can still hit it, but 2.1rem "Sol" and "Fa" sit very tight, and the check badge (22px, at top-right) crowds the text. Unverified at actual widths. -> Fix: below about 600px, use a 3+3+1 or 2-column layout, or lower the column count so each button is at least about 88px wide. Check that "Sol" does not touch the border.
4. [Minor] Home, Practice card (4+3 grid) - "Where's the last one?" The 7th note leaves an empty 8th cell. In the Qt grid the three bottom buttons are left-aligned and the same width as the top row, so the card looks unfinished. Notes also read left to right, then jump to a new line, which a pre-reader will not parse as a scale. -> Fix: center the bottom row, or use 3+3+1. Do not let the empty cell look like a missing or disabled button.
5. [Minor] Home, Practice card - Selected state in Qt. `QPushButton#note:checked` uses `ACCENT_SOFT` fill plus `ACCENT` border. The web has a check badge but the Qt button does not. Color and border weight alone could be missed by a color-blind child or from arm's length. -> Fix: add the same check mark (or a thicker border or scale-up) in Qt so selection matches the web.
6. [Minor] Home, Practice card - Hover and press feedback is a border color change only. A 3-5 child mashing the screen gets no obvious "pressed" cue. -> Fix: add a pressed state (slight scale or darker fill) on both platforms. Keep the motion short and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
7. [Minor] Home, Practice card - The note label is 40-42px display font plus a 20px letter line. The solfège is readable from arm's length. The letter name (C, D, E) is small and meaningless to a 3-5 child. That is fine for older bands, but it adds clutter at this age. -> Fix: acceptable. Keep it muted, as it is now.

**Would this child come back tomorrow?** Maybe. The big, round buttons are inviting to tap, and nothing here punishes them. But with 7 look-alike choices and no picture cues, they will depend on a grown-up to start. The change does not hurt a 3-5 child's first 10 seconds in itself, but it makes the Practice path less approachable than the old 3-note row.

## Cross-age summary
- Shared issues, ranked:
  1. Seven text-only choices (Major, 3-5, and probably 6-8 too, because they read slowly). Add a non-text cue per note.
  2. Narrow buttons on phones with 4 columns (Major, all bands, unverified).
  3. Unbalanced 4+3 grid and no pressed or selected parity between Qt and web (Minor).
- Conflicts between bands: 3-5 needs picture or shape cues on every note, while 12-15 may find them babyish. Recommended resolution: keep the cue small and abstract (a colored dot or staff mini-position) beside the solfège, rather than a cartoon. Older bands can still read the text first.
- Conflicts with the spec: none found. FR-12 (7 natural notes, C4-B4) and FR-11 (solfège plus letter) are met by the 7 buttons. Two things to flag, not change:
  - The Practice card text says "play it 10 times". I could not confirm that number against a spec requirement. It is unverified, and it is a very long task for 3-5 (FR-06 hold is 3 s each).
  - FR-12 does not say how notes should be laid out or whether non-text cues are allowed. Adding picture cues for pre-readers would need a docs change first.

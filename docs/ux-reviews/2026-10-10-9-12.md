# Child UX review: ages 9-12
Reviewed: Horn Hero v0.1 running build (12 screenshots driven by the keyboard simulator) plus ui/theme.py, ui/game_screen.py, ui/home_screen.py, ui/summary_screen.py, core/feedback.py, targeted greps of ui/settings_dialog.py and ui/main_window.py, and spec FR-03/06/07/16/18/21/22-25, NFR-01. Date: 2026-10-10.
Evidence limits: static screenshots and code only, not live interaction. No real horn or microphone input. The success chime cannot be heard. Animation and timing are inferred from code only (gauge ~15 ms tick with smoothing, hold ring fills over 3 s, 150 ms grace). Not seen in screenshots: the far-flat and near-sharp messages, a dropout/reset mid-hold, the reset-progress confirmation, any hover or focus state. The mockups in docs/design/ were not compared line by line.

## Age 9-12
**Verdict:** A confident reader understands the game in about 10 seconds and finds the practice screen clear and fair-looking, but the star rating feels arbitrary and the reward moments are thin for a child who wants to master something and beat a previous attempt.

**What works**
- Cover (02): one huge "Start Game!" button, one obvious next action. The "Warming up the horn..." pill with a progress bar (01) explains the wait.
- Home (03): one question, two clear cards, large Do/Ré/Mi tiles showing note name and letter, one primary button per card. Current level has a "Play" pill; locked levels are dashed and say "Locked" (not color alone). The star total "5 / 15" is a clear target.
- Practice (04-08): logical order (target note, staff, gauge, message). Flat / In tune / Sharp are labeled with symbol and text, and the active label becomes a highlighted chip, so state is never color alone. The dark needle is readable on every zone. Messages give an action ("Push a little higher!", "Relax and come down."), which this age can use immediately.
- Hold feedback is explicit: ring fills clockwise, notehead turns green, numeric "1.9 / 3.0 s". It reads as exact and fair. Point scored (08) shows a full ring, star icon, "Great job! +1 point!" and the score dots.
- Positive tone throughout (FR-18); no failure wording in any screenshot or in core/feedback.py (FR-16 respected).
- Silence (04) is a calm invitation: pale gauge, hollow needle, dashed ring, "Take a breath and play your Do!", "Listening...". It does not look like an error.
- Settings (12) is labeled "For grown-ups", saves immediately (FR-21), with plain wording and Easy/Normal/Strict presets.
- Back arrow and Escape on the practice screen; level complete offers Home / Play again / Next level with the next step highlighted.

**Problems**
1. [Major] Level complete (10) - "I got 2 stars. Why not 3? What did I do wrong?" The screen says "Try again to collect all 3 stars." but never says how stars are earned. In the spec stars come from "misses" (FR-22/FR-23: 0-2 misses = 3 stars), which the child never sees during play or afterwards. A 9-12 child will find this arbitrary and may suspect it is unfair. Likely behavior: replay without knowing what to change, or lose interest. -> Fix: summary_screen.show_level: add one positive reason line under the stars using the real miss count and the configured thresholds (not hard-coded), e.g. "You restarted a hold 4 times. Hold steady for 3 stars!". Optionally add a one-line star key to the Home level list. No change to FR-22/23 behavior; this only surfaces existing data.
2. [Major] Practice screen, flat and sharp states (05, 06) - "It says Flat with a down arrow, but the message has an up arrow. Which way do I go?" The gauge chip reads "↓ Flat" (your note is low) while the message icon directly below shows "↑" (go higher). Sharp is the same in reverse ("↑ Sharp" chip, "↓" message icon). Two opposite arrows on screen at once; arrows are read before text, especially at arm's length with a horn. -> Fix: theme.FEEDBACK_ICON and the gauge labels in ui/widgets.py: show only one direction symbol at a time. Keep the arrow in the message bar for the action, and change the gauge labels to words ("Low" / "In tune" / "High") or a marker that matches the needle side, with no vertical arrow. Test with children.
3. [Major] Practice screen, hold reset (not visible in screenshots; code and spec inference, unverified with a real horn) - "I was holding it and it went back to 0 for no reason." This age notices glitches and unfair resets. Grace is 150 ms and the hold restarts on leaving green (FR-07 default). Real horn tone has attack wobble and drift. Nothing on screen explains a reset: the message just becomes "Almost there! Push a little higher!" with "0.0 / 3.0 s". -> Fix: (a) when a hold longer than about 1 s is lost, show a short calm line in the message bar such as "So close! Start the hold again." for about 1.5 s before the normal flat/sharp text (positive wording, FR-18); (b) keep the 150 ms grace as a config constant and validate it against real horn recordings; (c) make the existing "Pause the 3-second timer instead of restarting" option easier for an adult to find (see Minor 6). Spec flag: FR-07 timing interacts with FR-22 miss counting.
4. [Major] Practice complete (11) - "That's it? I did 10 and just got a sentence." The reward is a title and one line on a mostly empty white card, with no visual reward and nothing to beat tomorrow. FR-24 rightly gives no stars, but this age is motivated by mastery. "10 perfect Do notes" is also inaccurate for a child who restarted several holds. -> Fix: summary_screen.show_practice: add a visual (large badge or the filled 10-dot row) and one personal-progress line stored in the single local profile (FR-25), e.g. "Most notes in a row without restarting: 6". Reword "perfect" to "steady" or "in tune". Do not add stars (FR-24).
5. [Major] Settings opened from the practice screen (code inference; the dialog itself is in 12; unverified live) - "I tapped the gear to see what it does." The gear sits in the practice top bar, same size and weight as Back, and a child can easily hit it. ui/main_window.py _open_settings does not stop the game timer (grep shows stop() only on back/finish), so a held note may complete behind the dialog, and changing tolerance mid-level changes star outcomes. -> Fix: pause the hold timer and gauge while the dialog is open and resume on Done; make the practice-screen gear smaller or secondary (or require a 1-second press) since the dialog is for grown-ups. Do not remove it (adults need it, spec Screen 1).

**Minor**
6. [Minor] Settings (12) - Two controls for one value sit side by side: the "Normal (±15 cents)" dropdown and an unlabeled "±15 cents" spin box. "cents" is jargon with no plain explanation. The Advanced checkbox label is long and sits under unrelated sliders; "Input level ... Quiet" has no instruction. -> Fix: label the spin box "Custom"; add one sentence under the dropdown ("Easy is more forgiving"); put the checkbox under an "Advanced" heading; add a "Play a note to test" hint to the input level bar.
7. [Minor] Practice screen (04-08) - "Hold for 3 seconds" and "Written pitch · Horn in F" captions are small grey text, hard to read at arm's length. "Written pitch" is jargon. -> Fix: raise captions to about 18-20 px; replace "Written pitch" with "Note on your music" and keep the technical wording in Settings.
8. [Minor] Cover (01, 02) - The cartoon child, mascot microphone and bunnies read young for the top of this band; 11-12-year-olds may call it babyish. Home and practice are clean and age-neutral. -> Fix: no change needed now; keep the cover as the only "young" screen.
9. [Minor] Home (03) - completed levels 1 and 2 show stars but no replay hint; only the current level has a Play pill, so a child wanting to improve 2 stars must guess the row is clickable. The Practice card has a large empty gap above Start practice. -> Fix: add a small "Replay" label to completed rows; tighten the gap.
10. [Minor] Far flat/sharp (06) - the whole message bar turns pink-red. Not failure wording, and FR-03 specifies red far zones on the gauge, but a red bar feels like an alarm. -> Fix: keep red on the gauge only and use the yellow bar for both near and far in ui/theme.py FEEDBACK_BACKGROUND, keeping the direction icon. This is a UI choice, not a spec change (FR-03 does not color the message bar).
11. [Minor] Level practice (09) - caption "LEVEL 2 · FIVE-NOTE SCALE" is small and there is no hint of the next note. Optional.
12. [Minor, verified by code only] The "Simulation: hold Space..." line is hidden unless the simulated source is active; confirm it never shows with a real microphone.
13. [Minor, unverified] Accessibility: needle and labels are not color-only (good); no flashing seen in code; success chime is not the only cue. Focus order, screen-reader reading of staff/gauge, and reduced-motion were not tested.

**Would this child come back tomorrow?** Maybe. The practice loop is clear and feels fair while playing, and levels plus a star goal give a reason to return. Without an explanation of stars (Problem 1) and a personal-progress reward (Problem 4), this child has little to beat except "get 3 stars somehow".

## Cross-age summary
Only age 9-12 was reviewed here; items for the other bands to check.
- Likely shared issues: the opposite-arrow clash (Problem 2) will hit 6-8 harder, because arrows are read before text; small captions (Minor 7) and the empty practice-complete screen (Problem 4) likely affect all bands; the hidden miss-based star rating (Problem 1) affects 6-8 and 12-15.
- Likely conflicts: the cartoon cover suits 6-8 and may feel young to 12-15; direction hints suit 6-8 and may over-explain for 12-15.
- Spec flags (do not change silently): FR-22/FR-23 miss-based stars are invisible to the child (Problem 1); FR-07 hold reset and the 150 ms grace interact with FR-22 miss counting (Problem 3); FR-03 colors the zones but not the message bar (Minor 10); FR-24 no stars in practice (Problem 4 respects it).
